Help for the DishNab app.

How do I import a recipe?

Open Nab a recipe. Paste a link and tap Nab it, or use Paste. For a normal recipe page, the phone fetches the page and reads it on the device. A public TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook link is requested through DishNab’s proxy. You can also tap Write manually, Choose a photo, or Take a photo. A photo is read on the phone and is not uploaded. Read the title, ingredients, and steps before you cook. An import can fail or come back incomplete.

Share sheet and the monthly limit

You can share a link into DishNab from another app. The share sheet saves the link on your phone. DishNab finishes the import when you open the app.

Social imports are free for 10 each calendar month. A social import counts when it saves a recipe with at least one ingredient. Website, typed, and photo imports are unlimited, and recipes you have saved stay available. When the 10 are used, the share sheet says so. You can save the link for later, or open DishNab and tap Upgrade. Pro is a one-time purchase from Apple, not a subscription. The count resets at the start of the next month.

Waiting to import

A social link that could not be imported because the monthly limit was reached can sit under Waiting to import. It stays until the month resets or you have Pro. Tap Import to try it, or Remove to drop it.

Restore purchases

In Settings, tap Restore Purchases. The purchase screen has the same button. Use the Apple ID you bought Pro with. Apple handles the purchase. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backup

In Settings, tap Export backup or Import backup. The JSON file is written on your device, and you choose where it goes. DishNab does not receive it. Anyone you share the file with can read it.

It includes recipes (including a photo saved with a recipe), folders, meal plans, and whether a recipe is still waiting to be filed. It does not include grocery items, import jobs, the social-import count, or a purchase.

Import checks the file first. It adds recipes and folders you do not already have, and it adds meal plans from the file. It skips a recipe with the same id or the same link, and it does not delete recipes already in your library.

iCloud

Recipes, folders, grocery items, meal plans, and import jobs are stored in your private iCloud when that store can be opened. The container is iCloud.com.noushadvali.dishnab. The developer cannot read that private data. If iCloud cannot be opened, they stay on the phone. If that also fails, the app shows: “Recipes will not be kept after you leave the app.”

Signing in to iCloud does not by itself mean a sync has finished. The monthly social-import count is also copied to your iCloud Key-Value Store. A JSON backup can move recipes, folders, and meal plans to another phone. It does not include grocery items.
