Support

Help for the DishNab app.

Email nouranbull@gmail.com.

Share a link

You can share a recipe link into DishNab. A normal recipe page, such as a blog, is read on your device. A public post on a social site the import proxy supports, such as TikTok, Instagram, or Facebook, is requested through DishNab’s proxy. A private or removed post may not import. DishNab is not those other apps.

Paste a link

You can paste a recipe link. Blog and other web recipes use the on-device reader. Supported social posts use the proxy. Either path can fail or come back incomplete.

Review an import

Before you cook, read the title, ingredients, and steps. The page you imported from may have been incomplete. Cooking mode shows the saved recipe. It does not fill missing steps or check allergies.

Restore a purchase

Restore a purchase from Settings with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Apple handles the payment. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Your recipes stay in the app, or in a backup you exported.

Backup and import

Export and import of a JSON backup are free. Export writes a file on your device, and you choose where it goes, such as Files, Mail, or AirDrop. Import reads a file you pick. DishNab does not receive the file. Anyone you send it to can read it.

Grocery list

The grocery list is free. It is a checklist of ingredients on this phone, in simple aisle groups, and it works offline. Items stay on the phone until you change them. During testing, iCloud sync may be unavailable, so the list may not appear on another phone yet. The list does not check allergies.

To file

To file is the inbox for imports you still need to organize. New imports can wait there. It is kept on this phone. During testing it may not appear on another device.

On this device

Search looks through recipes on the phone, not the whole web. Folders organize that library. Cooking mode shows a recipe you saved.

If you delete the app, recipes that exist only on that phone can go with it. Export a backup when you want a file you can import later.

iCloud

iCloud sync may be unavailable during testing. A recipe, grocery list, or To file inbox may not show on a second device yet. When you change phones, export a backup and import it on the new phone.
