The app

DishNab keeps recipes on your device. You can search, use folders, cook from a saved recipe, keep a free grocery list, and use To file to organize imports. Onboarding and Settings are included. Export and import of a JSON backup are free.

The grocery list is an offline checklist of ingredients on the phone, with simple aisle groups. To file is where imports can wait until you organize them. Undo, analytics, remote push notifications, and cloud AI are not part of this app.
