The app

DishNab keeps recipes for you. Search, folders, cooking mode, the grocery list, the weekly meal plan, and JSON backup are free.

Website imports, recipes you type, and photo imports are unlimited. Social imports from TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook are free for 10 each calendar month. A social import counts when it saves a recipe with at least one ingredient. Recipes you have saved are never locked.

Pro removes the monthly limit on social imports. It is an optional one-time purchase from Apple. It is not a subscription.

Cooking, allergies, and nutrition

A recipe can be wrong, incomplete, or unsafe for you. Nutrition figures in DishNab are estimates, or numbers that were already printed on the recipe. They are not medical advice, and they are not a checked nutrition label. DishNab does not check ingredients for allergies. You decide whether a recipe is right for you. Cooking mode shows a recipe you saved. The grocery list is a checklist you edit. It does not decide whether an item is safe.
