The app

DishNab is a place to keep recipes on your own device. This version includes search, folders, cooking mode, a free grocery list, a To file inbox, onboarding, Settings, and a free JSON backup you can export and import. The grocery list is an on-device checklist of ingredients with simple aisle groups. It is offline and local. To file is where imports sit until you organize them.
